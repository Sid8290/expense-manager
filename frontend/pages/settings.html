Help

How do I add a transaction?

Open the Transactions page, fill in the Add Transaction form and press Add Transaction.

Where is my data stored?

Everything is stored in this browser using localStorage. Nothing is sent to a server, and clearing your browser data removes it.

Why can't I delete an account or category?

Accounts and categories that are used by transactions cannot be deleted. Delete those transactions first.

What do the account types mean?

Cash, bank and online accounts hold your money and change with transactions. Assets, money to receive and money to give are tracked as fixed amounts.
